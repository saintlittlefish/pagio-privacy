InkWell Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

InkWell is a reading and writing practice tracker published by Xanadu Studios. This policy explains how InkWell handles information when you use the Android application.

Information you enter

InkWell lets you record book and project titles, optional comments and reflections, session times, pages read, words written, goals, and preferences. This information is stored locally on your device. Xanadu Studios does not operate a server that receives this information.

Information we collect or share

InkWell does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, or behavioral tracking.

Device backup and exports

Depending on your Android settings, the operating system may include InkWell data in an encrypted device or cloud backup managed by your device or backup provider. InkWell also lets you export CSV and JSON files. You choose where those files are saved and are responsible for protecting or deleting exported copies.

Permissions and notifications

InkWell requests notification permission so it can display an active timer and optional practice reminders. The app uses an Android foreground service while a user-started timer is active. These features do not collect or transmit personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or remove associated backups and exported files. Because Xanadu Studios does not receive your local data, we cannot retrieve or delete copies stored on your device or with your backup provider.

Children's privacy

InkWell is a general productivity application and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when InkWell's features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact Xanadu Studios at info@xanadustudiosmedia.com.
