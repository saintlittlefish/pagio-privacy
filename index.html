Pagio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Pagio is a reading and writing practice tracker published by Xanadu Studios. This policy explains how Pagio handles information when you use the Android application.

Information you enter

Pagio lets you record book and project titles, optional comments and reflections, session times, pages read, words written, goals, and preferences. Pagio stores this information locally on your device. If you choose to sign in and use cloud sync, Pagio also stores your session history and books or projects in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so they can be available on your other signed-in devices.

Optional accounts and cloud sync

You can use Pagio without creating an account. If you choose to create an account or sign in, Google Firebase Authentication processes account information such as your user ID, email address, and, for Google sign-in, your display name. Email/password users must verify their email address before cloud synchronization begins.

Cloud-synchronized information is associated with your Firebase user ID and is accessible only to authenticated requests for that account. Pagio uses this information to provide account management, authentication, backup, and synchronization across your devices.

Service providers and sharing

Pagio uses Google Firebase as a service provider for optional authentication and cloud synchronization. Data is transmitted to Firebase only when these features require it. Xanadu Studios does not sell or rent your information and does not share it for advertising. Pagio does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, or behavioral tracking. Google's handling of information is governed by its applicable terms and privacy policies.

Device backup and exports

Depending on your Android settings, the operating system may include Pagio data in an encrypted device or cloud backup managed by your device or backup provider. Pagio also lets you export CSV and JSON files. You choose where those files are saved and are responsible for protecting or deleting exported copies.

Permissions and notifications

Pagio requests notification permission so it can display an active timer and optional practice reminders. The app uses an Android foreground service while a user-started timer is active. These features do not collect or transmit personal information.

Security

Pagio uses encrypted network connections when communicating with Firebase. Cloud Firestore access rules restrict each signed-in user to their own synchronized data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or remove associated backups and exported files. Signing out does not delete the local copy on that device.

If you use an account, synchronized data remains in Firebase until you delete individual records or use Delete account and cloud data in Pagio's Account and sync settings. That action deletes the account and its synchronized Pagio data. Copies previously downloaded to another device remain locally on that device until removed there. You may also contact us for privacy or deletion assistance.

Children's privacy

Pagio is a general productivity application and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Pagio's features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published.
